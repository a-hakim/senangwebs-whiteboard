SenangWebs Works (SWW) - Digital Whiteboard & Vector Drawing Tool

Quick Start Guide:

  • Tools: Select from toolbar (left side)
  • Pan: Alt+Click and drag or Middle mouse
  • Zoom: Mouse wheel
  • Select: Click objects or drag selection box
  • Multi-select: Shift+Click
  • Delete: Select objects and press Delete
  • Properties: Adjust colors, stroke, fill (right panel)
  • Text: Click Text tool, then click canvas to add text
  • Export: Use export buttons in toolbar
